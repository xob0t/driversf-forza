First release. It converts the cars from your own Forza Horizon and Forza
Motorsport 4 discs (Xbox 360) and adds them to Driver: San Francisco 1.04 on PC:
573 cars, each with its own garage card, maker group, HUD badge and name.

## Install

1. Download `driversf-forza-v0.1.0.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Put your Forza `.iso` files in its `assets` folder.
3. Close the game and double-click `install.cmd`.

What you need (DSF 1.04, the disc images, the Visual C++ x86 runtime, disk
space) is in the [README](https://github.com/xob0t/driversf-forza#readme). The
first run builds every car on your PC, which takes one to a few hours. To
remove everything, run `.\install.cmd --uninstall` in the unzipped folder.
[INSTALL.md](https://github.com/xob0t/driversf-forza/blob/v0.1.0/INSTALL.md)
lists every file the installer changes and what it does to your save.

## What the cars have

- Forza's body, cockpit, glass, lamps, wheels and paint, on a similar stock car
  for damage, animation and handling.
- Stock-style damage: panels, crush and parts that come off.
- Forza's own engine sound.
- Working speedometer and rev counter needles in the cockpit view on most cars.
- Garage pictures, maker logos, HUD badges and number plates.

## Known issues

This is a 0.x pre-release: everything drives, but some cars still look wrong
in places.

- Some headlight and tail-lamp glass doesn't look like Forza's (#89), and
  pop-up headlamps show raised and lowered at once (#88).
- Satin-black trims (scoops, grilles) draw flat black (#58, #87).
- About 60 cars with digital dashboards have no working gauge (#63).
- On some cars the tyres touch the arch at full lock (#54).
- A few cars have no exhaust smoke or smoke from the host's tailpipes (#64, #65).
- Seven cars show a different steering wheel in the chase view (#86).

The issue list has the rest:
<https://github.com/xob0t/driversf-forza/issues>.
