Fixes from the first players' reports and more testing. Every car is converted again on the first run of this version (one to a few hours).

## Update

Download `driversf-forza-v0.1.1.zip` below, unzip it, put your Forza `.iso` files in its `assets` folder, close the game and run `install.cmd`. It replaces the earlier install; your save is backed up first.

## Fixes

- **Disc images are recognised by what's on them, not by their file names.** With only the FM4 or only the FH1 discs, those cars now get their garage pictures, badges, names and engine sounds (#113, #102).
- **A car never shows another car's nameplate** (the Ford Ka showed "SHELBY GT500"), and the installer refuses a build that is missing its garage and HUD files (#98).
- **Head lamps glow by day, like stock cars,** on 549 of the 573 cars, up from 64.
- **Brake lights:** the Lotus Carlton's work, and the visible brake area is larger on 107 cars.
- **Lamp lenses look like glass:** the reflector and bulb show through, as in Forza (Lancer Evo VI, Carlton, Esperante GTLM and 484 cars in all), and the WRX STI's tail lamps glow evenly.
- **Rims** have Forza's brightness (the Carlton's were bright white; 508 cars).
- **Trims:** grille detail and mid-grey plastics show instead of flat black (553 cars).
- **The F355 Challenge's tail panel** and other recessed panels keep their plain trim instead of a blurred picture.
- **Steering wheels turn about their own hub** instead of swinging (the Regal GNX).
- **Nameplates show accented letters and punctuation:** REVENTÓN, MURCIÉLAGO, CEE'D, #1.

## Known issues

- Satin-black trims (scoops, grilles) still draw flat black (#87), and pop-up headlamps show raised and lowered at once (#88).
- The Lotus Carlton's head lamps look like white blobs by day (#115).
- About 60 cars with digital dashboards have no working gauge (#63).
- Hands sit up to a few cm off some steering wheels (fixed for the next release: #118, #117).

All open issues: <https://github.com/xob0t/driversf-forza/issues>.
