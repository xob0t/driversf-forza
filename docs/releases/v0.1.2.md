Lamps, gloss, hands on the wheel, working digital dashboards and Forza's steering. Every car is converted again on the first run of this version (one to a few hours); later runs only redo what changed.

## Update

Download `driversf-forza-v0.1.2.zip` below, unzip it, put your Forza `.iso` files in its `assets` folder, close the game and run `install.cmd`. It replaces the earlier install; your save is backed up first.

## Fixes and changes

- **Lamps break with their own end of the car.** Head lamps no longer break in rear collisions, and tail lamps no longer in front ones (head lamps on 465 cars, tail and brake lamps on 317) (#132, #141). That is also why some cars' tail lamps looked cracked or stayed dark when braking (Veyron, Cee'd).
- **The X5 M's tail glass** and 13 other cars' lens patches keep Forza's flat tint instead of a mosaic (#141).
- **Head lamps glow like stock cars' running lamps:** from the lamp and its reflector, hot in the middle and dark at the rim; the M3 E30's round lamps glow (#124, #115).
- **Pop-up head lamps** are drawn lowered only, instead of raised and lowered at once (#88).
- **Gloss on trims and rims:** satin-black scoops and grilles have Forza's sheen instead of flat black, chrome rims reflect (#87, #108).
- **Hands on the wheel:** the driver's hands grip Forza's steering wheel in the cockpit, chase views and on AI drivers (#118, #117).
- **Digital dashboards** work on about 300 cars: speed, rpm, gear and needles on Forza's own displays (#63).
- **Steering lock** is Forza's for every car, so tyres no longer clip the arches at full lock; tight low-speed turns are as tight as in Forza (#54).
- **Install:**
  - `install.cmd --report` makes a zip to attach to a bug report.
  - A re-run with nothing changed re-packs in seconds (#100).
  - A car that converts slightly differently on a re-run no longer stops the build (#133).
  - DriverNGHook is unpacked without Windows' `tar.exe`, whose LZMA support some Windows builds lack (#140).

## Known issues

- Pop-up head lamps stay lowered when the lights are on (#122).
- From the driver's seat, the bonnet scoop and A-pillars have no gloss (#136).
- Model nameplates are plain chrome text, not the car's own lettering (#139).
- Some reverse and fog lamps still break with the other end of the car (#146).
- The steering wheel drawn in the chase views is off-centre on seven cars (#86).
- 7 open-cockpit racers whose only display is on the steering wheel, and 15 cars with a placeholder cockpit, have no working gauge (#147, #148).
